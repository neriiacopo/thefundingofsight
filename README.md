# Fields of Vision

_Fields of Vision_ is an exploration of the relationship between state-of-the-art computer vision research and its real-world deployment.

The project begins with an analysis of the sponsors of the three major computer vision conferences—CVPR, ICCV, and ECCV, grouped into sectors in order to reveal the industrial landscape surrounding contemporary computer vision research. The analysis of the same landscape is further enriched by a journalistic corpus assembled from WIRED's RSS following the _artificial intelligence_ tag. Through semantic matching,representative articles are selected to trace how computer vision technologies materialize as products, infrastructures, policies, controversies, and social transformations.

The resulting visualization juxtaposes benchmark-driven research and its public consequences, mapping the circulation of computer vision across industries, institutions, and everyday life.
